Frykin Industries is an engineering and manufacturing consultancy I founded in 2022. It's where the software half of my work meets the hardware half.

## What it does

- **EV conversion electronics** — custom PCB drive controllers communicating over CAN bus for electric-vehicle conversions.
- **Advanced 3D printing** — a working additive-manufacturing facility for prototyping and production parts.
- **Reverse engineering** — recovering and re-modeling legacy automotive components in Fusion 360 when no drawings survive.

## Why it matters to me

Two decades of platform engineering taught me how distributed software systems behave. Frykin keeps me honest about the physical layer — tolerances, signal integrity, the parts of a system you can hold in your hand. Hardware doesn't let you paper over a bad design with a retry loop.

It is a side venture, not a day job — but it is the most direct expression of how I like to work: find a hard problem, learn what I don't know, build the thing.
